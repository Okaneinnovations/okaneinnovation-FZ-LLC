Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or interact with the website of Okane Innovations FZ-LLC.

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Okane Innovations FZ-LLC respects the privacy of visitors to its website and is committed to handling information responsibly.

This Privacy Policy describes the general approach to information that may be collected through this website and how such information may be used.

2. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you interact with the website, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Your name or business name.
  • Your email address.
  • Information included in a business enquiry.
  • Other information that you choose to provide when contacting us.

3. Information Collected Automatically

Like many websites, basic technical information may be processed by hosting, security, analytics or other technical services used to operate the website.

Such information may include technical identifiers, browser information, device information, approximate usage information and website access information, depending on the services enabled on the website.

4. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for legitimate business and operational purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Communicating about requested services or information.
  • Maintaining and improving the website.
  • Protecting the website and its users against misuse or security threats.
  • Meeting applicable legal or regulatory obligations.

5. Email Communications

If you contact Okane Innovations through the email address displayed on this website, information contained in your communication may be used to respond to your enquiry and manage the related business communication.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is specifically necessary for your enquiry.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies if such functionality is enabled through hosting, analytics, security or third-party services.

Where applicable, your browser may provide controls for managing or restricting cookies.

7. Third-Party Services

The website may use third-party infrastructure or services for hosting, security, analytics, communication or other operational purposes.

Those third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect information handled through the website.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain business records, resolve enquiries, protect legitimate interests, or comply with applicable legal obligations.

10. Your Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion or other forms of control.

Requests can be directed to the contact email provided below.

11. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business and general informational purposes and is not specifically directed toward children.

12. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the website, business operations, technology or applicable requirements.

The updated version will be published on this page with a revised date where appropriate.
